Once you close the deal: publish it free

The exported ZIP is a complete website you can put online for free — no coding. Full step-by-step is in the DEPLOY.md file inside the ZIP. Quick version:

  1. Export the website ZIP above and unzip it.
  2. Get the real details from your new client (phone, address, hours, services, photos) and replace the draft placeholders in index.html.
  3. Put it online free — the easiest way: go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the folder in. You instantly get a live link like their-shop.netlify.app. (Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages also work, all free.)
  4. Contact form already works for free — it's wired to FormSubmit. Set the client's email in the demo's Email box before exporting; they confirm it once and every enquiry emails them automatically.
  5. Custom domain (optional): a domain is ~$10/year (Namecheap, Cloudflare, Porkbun); hosting stays free. Point it at the host in its "custom domain" settings.

So your only possible cost is a domain name if the client wants one — the website and hosting are 100% free.
